## How to become a maintainer
The path from community member to maintainer is described in the [Contributor ladder](./GOVERNANCE.md#contributor-ladder) in GOVERNANCE.md.

## Stepping down / emeritus (proposed)
Maintainers who wish to step down can do so by opening a PR to remove themselves from this file and the appropriate teams. They may retain Emeritus status (honorary mention with no permissions) at the discretion of the remaining maintainers. After **12 months** of inactivity, a maintainer may be transitioned to Emeritus by a maintainer team decision.
